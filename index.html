How it works

Six easy steps

  1. 01

    Choose

    Pick a card layout and how many cards to print.

  2. 02

    Pay

    Scan a PromptPay QR code. PromptPay QR payment can be added to your booth; the demo uses test money.

  3. 03

    Pose

    A live camera view and a countdown take the photos.

  4. 04

    Arrange

    Drag the photos onto the cards.

  5. 05

    Decorate

    Add text, drawings, stickers and filters to every print.

  6. 06

    Print

    The cards are printed at the booth.

Questions

What size are the prints?

The card is designed as a 6 x 4 inch postcard for every layout.

How many people fit in a photo?

SayCheer is designed for groups of 1 to 10 people.

How do guests pay?

By scanning a PromptPay QR code. This can be added to your booth; the demo uses test money.

Are the photos stored?

Photos stay in the booth's memory during the session and are discarded when it ends.

Which languages do the screens use?

Thai, with an English option.

Can it carry my shop's look?

Your name, logo and colours can be added for your shop.
