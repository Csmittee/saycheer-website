How it works

Six easy steps

  1. Choose

    Pick a card layout and how many cards to print.

  2. Pay

    Scan a PromptPay QR code. PromptPay QR payment can be added to your booth; the demo uses test money.

  3. Pose

    A live camera view and a countdown take the photos.

  4. Arrange

    Drag the photos onto the cards.

  5. Decorate

    Add text, drawings, stickers and filters to every print.

  6. Print

    The cards are printed at the booth.
